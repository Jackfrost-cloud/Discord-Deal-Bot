# Privacy Policy — FreeDeals

**Last updated:** March 22, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FreeDeals ("we", "our", "the bot") collects, uses, and stores information when you use our Discord bot. By using FreeDeals, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## 2. Data We Collect

When you interact with FreeDeals, we may collect and store the following data:

- **Discord User ID** — collected when you subscribe to automatic deal notifications via the bot's subscription feature.
- **Command usage** — we may log when commands are used for the purpose of debugging and improving the service. We do not log message content.

We do not collect your username, email address, IP address, or any other personal information beyond what is listed above.

## 3. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used solely for the following purposes:
- To send you automatic deal reports via Discord direct message, if you have subscribed
- To manage and maintain your subscription status
- To improve the reliability and performance of the bot

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party.

## 4. Data Retention

Your Discord User ID is retained for as long as you remain subscribed to FreeDeals notifications. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at **freedeals13@outlook.com**.

## 5. Future Paid Features

A paid subscription tier may be introduced in the future. If payment processing is added, it will be handled by a third-party payment provider. At that time, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly. You will be informed before any such changes take effect.

## 6. Third-Party Services

FreeDeals fetches publicly available information from third-party platforms including Epic Games, Steam, GOG, Reddit, and others. We do not share your personal data with these platforms.

## 7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the data we store. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

## 8. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Know what data we hold about you
- Request correction or deletion of your data
- Unsubscribe from notifications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **freedeals13@outlook.com**.

## 9. Children's Privacy

FreeDeals is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be communicated via the bot or its support channels. Continued use of FreeDeals after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
